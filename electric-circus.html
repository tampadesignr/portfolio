Electric Circus

This was one of my first big fine art painting, The picture of it on the back of a van gives you an idea of its size. I put it on the back of the van to take a picture of it in natural light. The painting is mixed media and was difficult to capture with a camera because if its size and how the light interacted withe the different sheens of the different mediums used. This one was a challenge for AI to work with and I will need to return to it at a later time. AI in general has a difficult time with objects in proximity that are not related. The test images were still fun and sharing the beginning of my journey with my original artworks and transforming them in different way. I thought it would be fun for funky characters to take a selfie with the work.
