Woman Resting on Chair

This is a sketch that was unplanned, a surprise, and my first time drawing somebody posing just for me. I drove some Navy buddies home a few states away and one of them knew I liked to draw and he had a surprise for me. He leads me up stairs to a apartment and tells me this girl is for you to draw, well this was a surprise, I was not expecting this and honestly one of the coolest surprises anyone has given me. She was sitting in the room just as I drew her, she did ask me what I wanted her to do and I said as you are is fine. I will admit I could have done better but I just drove for 6 hours and was a little tired but was passing up the experience. This was fun to try different AI styles converting the image to a finished art piece.
